## v1.0.1
- Tweaked damage and durability of the Staff of Storms and Wrath of Muspelheim to be more in line with other staves

## v1.0.0
- Staff of Storms, a lightning staff made at the Galdr Table: its attack is a lightning bolt that hits at once where you aim, chaining to nearby enemies more often as it's refined, and its secondary attack (from level 5) calls lightning down from the sky on the spot you aim at. Magic Supremacy's Thundersurge model and effects, and the Obliterator's lightning
- Wrath of Muspelheim, a fire staff made at the Galdr Table: its attack is a fire beam that hits at once where you aim and blasts what's around, and its secondary attack (from level 5) is a flamethrower. Magic Supremacy's Incinerator model and effects
- Both refine at the Forge of Potential up to level 20, their look and effects growing with each level
