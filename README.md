# NhxMagicWeapons

A private mod for our server: magic weapons made with Magic Supremacy's models and effects (https://valheim.hexium.gg/mods/Dreanegade/Magic_Supremacy). Not published.

## Staff of Storms

A lightning staff, made at the Galdr Table from 20 Yggdrasil wood, 16 refined eitr and 2 thunder stones (Haldor sells them). Each level up to 4 takes 10 more Yggdrasil wood, 8 eitr and 1 thunder stone, and like the game's Mistlands staves, it can be refined further at the Forge of Potential with black marble battle idols. It uses Elemental Magic.

- **Attack: lightning bolt.** A bolt leaps from the staff's prongs and hits the first thing where you aim, up to 50 m, at once: no projectile to dodge or lead. 150 lightning damage (+6 per level) to one target, for 25 eitr. It goes through your friends and tames.
- **Secondary attack (middle mouse): call lightning.** Raise the staff and lightning strikes from the sky on the spot you aim at, up to 60 m (on the ground under it, if you aim at a creature or the sky). Twice the bolt's damage to everything within 3.5 m, for 50 eitr.

Neither hurts you, your friends or your tames (unless you've turned PvP on), and neither damages buildings.

The staff is Magic Supremacy's Thundersurge; the bolt's sparks and impact are Magic Supremacy's, the strike is the Obliterator's lightning.

## Installing

Everyone on the server needs it, with the same version: the server, and every player. Copy the NhxMagicWeapons folder into BepInEx\plugins. It needs Jotunn.

It can't run with Magic Supremacy: it uses Magic Supremacy's asset bundle, which Unity can't load twice. With both installed, BepInEx doesn't load this mod.
